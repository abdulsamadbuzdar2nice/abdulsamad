About Me

I am an academic and researcher specializing in Food Science and Technology, with a particular focus on Meat Science, poultry meat processing, product development, quality improvement, and sustainable food innovation. I hold an MS in Applied Life Science (Meat Science and Technology) and I am currently pursuing a PhD in Applied Life Science (Meat Science and Technology) as a Brain Korea Scholarship recipient.

My research interests include meat and poultry processing technologies, hybrid and alternative meat products, functional food development, protein structuring, meat quality evaluation, sensory analysis, alternative ingredients, and value-added food product development.
